At the table

How do I use the dice in solo mode?

The shared dice button is centered below your player tile and above the advertising area. Tap to roll. Double tap or hold to choose D20, D6, D4 or a coin.

Where are my match and playmats saved?

They are saved in the app's private storage on your device. SpellCount has no publisher cloud-save account. Avoid clearing app storage or uninstalling if you need to preserve your game and images. Contact support before taking a destructive troubleshooting step.

Why will my image or GIF not import?

Select an individual image through the Android system picker. Static input is limited to 32 MB. Animated GIFs must be no larger than 4 MB or 512 × 512 pixels, with at most 240 frames and within the app's decoded animation budget. Unsupported or incomplete files may be rejected.

Does removing a playmat delete my original photo?

No. Removing a tile's playmat does not delete the original selected photo. Private copies may remain because a saved preset or Undo entry can still refer to them.

Can I use SpellCount offline?

Core life, counter, dice and other tracking tools work offline. Advertising and public web pages need connectivity. The full privacy policy is also available offline in Settings.

Where can I change advertising choices?

Open Settings → Ad Preferences to change your age-group choice. Google's Privacy options entry appears in Settings where required by the consent service. App age preferences do not replace Google's applicable consent controls.

Why does the screen stay awake?

Keep Screen Awake is enabled by default while the app is visible. You can disable it in Settings. It does not prevent manual locking, and normal timeout behavior returns when the app is backgrounded.

Tell us what happened.

Email cardboardsquire@gmail.com with your device model, Android version, SpellCount version and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help, but remove sensitive details before sending it.

Support messages are retained for up to one month after resolution, subject to the exceptions described in the policy. Do not send passwords, signing keys or unrelated personal information.
