SpellCount Data Controls

SpellCount does not require an account. We do not store your saved games in a Cardboard Squire cloud service.

Delete saved games and images

Your saved games, game history, Undo history, settings and imported playmats are stored on your device.

To delete all of SpellCount’s local data, open:

Android Settings → Apps → SpellCount → Storage → Clear storage

The wording may be slightly different on your phone.

This permanently deletes the app’s local data, including your saved games and imported images. Save anything you want to keep before continuing.

Removing a playmat selection inside SpellCount may leave copies or references in saved presets or history. Clearing local storage does not delete information Google has already received.

Remove an imported playmat

Open the player’s Appearance menu and choose Remove image. This restores the selected tile background color. It does not delete the original photo or GIF from your phone.

Private copies may still be used by a saved appearance preset or Undo history. To remove all of SpellCount’s local copies, use Android’s Clear storage option above after saving anything you want to keep.

Delete messages you've sent us

You can ask us to delete support emails or feedback-form submissions you previously sent to Cardboard Squire. Email cardboardsquire@gmail.com and tell us which messages you want deleted. For form submissions, include the submission reference or approximate date and message details. Please write from the original email address where possible.

Do not send passwords, payment information or identity documents.

We keep support emails for up to one month after your request is resolved, then delete them from active support mailboxes unless a legal obligation requires us to keep them longer. Our email and form-processing providers may retain submissions, logs, backup or security copies under their applicable policies and plan. We do not promise deletion of all provider records on the same schedule.

Google advertising data

SpellCount uses Google advertising and consent services. Google may process advertising, diagnostic and device information through those services. This is separate from the saved games on your phone and emails held by Cardboard Squire.

Uninstalling SpellCount, clearing its storage or emailing us does not mean that Google’s retained information is deleted. We have not verified a way for Cardboard Squire to delete all Google SDK records on your behalf.

For Google’s data practices and available advertising controls, visit the Google Privacy Policy and My Ad Center. Changing advertising settings or resetting an advertising identifier does not establish that previously retained information has been erased.
