SpellCount — Privacy Policy
Effective date: November 9, 2026
Publisher: Cardboard Squire
Contact: cardboardsquire@gmail.com

SpellCount is a tabletop game tracker. It does not require an online account. This policy describes the app distributed by Cardboard Squire, including its advertising and consent services, and the support website feedback form.

1. Game information and imported playmats
Player labels, life totals, counters, commander damage, tokens, mana, reminders, settings, appearance presets and Undo history are saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app does not send these game records to Cardboard Squire or attach them to advertising requests.

When you select an image or GIF through Android's system picker, SpellCount copies that selected file into private app storage. Images may be resized or converted for performance. The app does not request access to your entire photo library and does not upload your playmats. Selected image files may contain information supplied by you, so choose what you import with care.

2. Advertising and Google's services
SpellCount uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads are requested as non-personalized for everyone. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed: Google's services can process IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from IP), device or app identifiers where permitted, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Cardboard Squire does not operate a separate game-data advertising or analytics server.

SpellCount does not request Android Advertising ID or Android AdServices identifier, attribution or Topics permissions. The SDK's age-treatment settings are also applied to restrict identifier use and advertising treatment. Google controls its services and retention practices; see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and advertising information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Age choices, children and consent
SpellCount offers a neutral, optional age-group choice before requesting ads. It stores the choice on your device, not a date of birth. The choice is used to set child-directed and under-age-of-consent signals in requests to Google's services. These signals can reveal a broad age category to Google; an exact birth date is not requested or sent by SpellCount.

Users who choose Under 18 and users who do not answer receive child-directed ad treatment. Because this simplified choice does not distinguish children from teenagers, all minors receive the stricter treatment. Ad content is requested with Google's general-audience maximum rating. Full-screen interstitial ads are disabled for children, teenagers and unknown-age users. Adults may see interstitials only at existing natural game transitions, subject to consent and ad availability. These settings do not verify a person's age.

UMP checks whether privacy messages or consent are required for a request. If the app's consent update or form handling fails, ads stay disabled and gameplay remains available. Where Google requires a privacy-options entry, it appears in Settings. You can change your age-group choice in Settings > Ad Preferences. Changing the choice refreshes the app's consent and ad-request gates. Advertising and consent availability depend on Google's services and applicable regional settings.

Cardboard Squire does not knowingly request children's personal information through an online game account or publisher game-data upload. Parents or guardians can contact cardboardsquire@gmail.com with privacy questions. Local game labels and playmats are controlled on the device; they are not a publisher cloud profile.

4. Local retention and deletion
Local information remains until removed through app controls, Android's Clear storage option or ordinary uninstallation. Starting a new match and using Undo can retain previous information, player profiles and appearance references. Removing a playmat from a tile does not necessarily erase every private copy because a saved preset or Undo entry may still reference it. Clearing the app's storage or ordinarily uninstalling it removes the app's local data; back up anything you need before taking those actions.

SpellCount disables Android cloud backup and requests exclusion of its private game data from Android backup and device-transfer facilities. Manufacturer-specific transfer tools can have their own behavior. Cardboard Squire has no game-save cloud copy to recover or delete. Google retains advertising and consent data according to its own policies and settings.

5. Support correspondence
If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer the request and troubleshoot the issue. Support messages are retained for up to one month after the request is resolved, then deleted from active support mailboxes unless a legal obligation requires longer retention. The email provider can retain backup or security copies under its own policies. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for support. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence.

Website feedback form
The support form is optional and does not require a visitor account. When you submit it, we receive your selected category, message, submission time, submission reference and reply email if you provide one. Do not send sensitive personal information. We use this information to handle support, investigate bugs and consider improvements, not to add you to a marketing list. We cannot reply if you leave the email field blank.

Formspree, Inc. processes submissions and sends notification emails to Cardboard Squire. Formspree may process IP addresses, browser information, access times and related technical information for service operation and spam prevention; processing may take place in the United States or other countries where the provider operates. Formspree and any enabled spam-verification provider apply their own privacy and security practices. See https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/ . If Google reCAPTCHA is used for spam verification, see https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/terms .

We retain support copies for up to one month after resolution, subject to the legal-obligation exception above. Provider-held submissions, logs and backups follow the provider's applicable plan and retention practices; this is not a promise to erase every provider record on that schedule. You can request deletion of support email and form submissions by contacting cardboardsquire@gmail.com and providing the submission reference or enough details to locate the message. No reply address is required to submit, but omission may make later verification of a deletion request harder. GitHub Pages hosts the static website; it does not itself maintain a feedback-submission inbox. A mailto link opens your email client and sends nothing until you send the email there.

6. Security and device access
Game saves and imported files use Android's app-private storage. Network and consent services use their providers' transmission protections. No storage system or network service can be guaranteed completely secure. SpellCount does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location permissions. Keep Screen Awake uses Android's visible-window setting and does not prevent manual locking.

7. Availability, choices and policy updates
The app is intended for users in the countries and age groups selected in its store listing, subject to store availability and applicable restrictions. Core tracking and the system image picker can be used without an online game account. Advertising services require connectivity; gameplay does not.

Google's privacy controls and applicable regional rights are described in its policies. For questions about Cardboard Squire's handling of support correspondence or this app, contact cardboardsquire@gmail.com. Material policy changes will be reflected in the policy's effective date and the policy available in the app and on its public policy page.
