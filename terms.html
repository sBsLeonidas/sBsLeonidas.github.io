Using SpellCount

SpellCount is a manual tabletop companion for one to six players. You control the life totals, counters, tokens, mana and other entries. Optional turn tools and reminders assist you; the app does not automatically detect the physical game state or replace the rules of a game. Verify important entries and resolve game disagreements with the people at your table.
